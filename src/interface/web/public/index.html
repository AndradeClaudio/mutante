Telegraph testnet · Hackathon Season II · for judges and testers

Crash-test the reviewers
with your own code.

Paste one small JavaScript function with its contract in the first comment, and the tests that prove it. Mutante plants bugs in it, keeps only the bugs your tests prove, buys a review of each one from Telegraph's code-review miners, and shows who caught what and what each correct answer cost.

Your code is sent to Telegraph miners and becomes public on a testnet. Do not paste anything private. A run buys about 20 reviews at $0.01 each in test USDC, paid by this service's testnet wallet, and takes one to two minutes.
